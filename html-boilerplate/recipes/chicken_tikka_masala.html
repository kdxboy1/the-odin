Classic Chicken Tikka Masala

Chicken tikka masala is a vibrant and deeply flavorful dish featuring marinated, roasted pieces of chicken bathed in a rich, spiced curry sauce. The traditional recipe highlights tender chicken infused with yogurt and spices, simmered in a creamy tomato-based gravy. Once plated, it boasts a striking orange-red hue and a tantalizing aroma that promises a warm, comforting meal.

This iconic dish represents a beautiful fusion of culinary traditions, generally traced back to South Asian chefs in the United Kingdom. While authentic Indian curries rely on a complex, slow-cooked base, this British-Indian restaurant staple incorporates cream or coconut milk to mellow the spices for a broader palate. Today, it stands as a globally beloved classic that perfectly balances robust heat with velvety smoothness.

Ingredients

  • Protein: Boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • Marinade: Plain yogurt, lemon juice, and ginger-garlic paste
  • Sauce: Tomato purée and heavy cream
  • Aromatics: Onion, garlic, and fresh cilantro
  • Spices: Garam masala, turmeric, cumin, and paprika

Steps

  1. Marinate chicken: Coat chicken pieces in yogurt and spices, resting for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Sear protein: Cook the marinated chicken in a skillet until browned, then set aside.
  3. Build flavor: Sauté chopped onions and garlic until golden and fragrant.
  4. Simmer sauce: Stir in tomato purée, heavy cream, and remaining spices, bringing to a gentle simmer.
  5. Combine dish: Return the chicken to the sauce and simmer for 15 minutes until fully cooked.
  6. Garnish and serve: Top with freshly chopped cilantro and serve warm alongside steamed basmati rice.
