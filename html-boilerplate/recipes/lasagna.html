Easy Homemade Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked dish made of stacked layers of wide, flat pasta sheets alternating with rich fillings. The traditional recipe features a savory meat ragù, creamy béchamel white sauce, and cheeses like mozzarella and Parmigiano-Reggiano. Once assembled, it is baked in an oven until the top layer turns bubbly, golden brown, and perfectly melted.

This comforting meal originated in Italy and boasts numerous regional variations. For example, northern styles emphasize delicate spinach egg pasta and béchamel, while southern versions often incorporate ricotta, sliced hard-boiled eggs, or meatballs. Today, it remains a globally beloved culinary staple that balances savory flavors with a hearty, satisfying texture.

Ingredients

  • Pasta: Lasagna sheets
  • Meat: Ground beef
  • Sauce: Marinara or tomato sauce
  • Dairy: Ricotta, mozzarella, and parmesan cheese
  • Aromatics: Garlic and onion
  • Seasoning: Salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Brown meat: Cook ground beef with chopped onion and garlic in a pan.
  2. Simmer sauce: Stir marinara sauce and Italian seasoning into the meat.
  3. Mix cheese: Combine ricotta cheese with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  4. Layer ingredients: Spread meat sauce in a baking dish, then alternate layers of pasta, ricotta, and mozzarella.
  5. Bake dish: Cover with foil and bake at 190°C (375°F) for 25 minutes.
  6. Brown top: Remove foil, top with parmesan, and bake 10 minutes more until bubbly.
