Traditional Beef Stroganoff

Beef Stroganoff is a hearty and luxurious Russian dish consisting of sautéed pieces of tender beef served in a rich, tangy sauce. The traditional recipe relies on high-quality cuts of meat quickly seared, combined with earthy mushrooms, and folded into a velvety sour cream gravy. Once finished, it delivers a deeply savory and satisfying flavor profile that warms the soul.

This comforting classic originated in mid-19th-century Russia, named after the influential Stroganov family. Early versions simply paired beef with a basic mustard and sour cream bouillon, but it evolved over time to include onions and mushrooms as it spread across Europe and the Americas. Today, it remains a beloved international staple, typically served over a bed of broad egg noodles or crisp potato straws depending on the region.

Ingredients

  • Meat: Thinly sliced beef sirloin or tenderloin
  • Produce: Cremini mushrooms and yellow onions
  • Dairy: Smetana (or full-fat sour cream) and butter
  • Base: Rich beef broth and a touch of Dijon mustard
  • Aromatics: Fresh thyme and garlic
  • Base carbohydrate: Broad egg noodles

Steps

  1. Sear beef: Quickly brown the beef slices in butter over high heat, then remove from the pan.
  2. Sauté vegetables: Cook sliced onions and mushrooms in the remaining drippings until softened.
  3. Create base: Stir in garlic, thyme, and beef broth, scraping up any browned bits from the pan.
  4. Thicken sauce: Whisk in a dollop of Dijon mustard and reduce the heat to low.
  5. Incorporate dairy: Gently fold in the sour cream until the sauce is smooth, avoiding a rolling boil.
  6. Combine and serve: Toss the beef back into the sauce to warm through, then ladle generously over egg noodles.
